Background, objectives and the people behind the 1st Annual Scientific Conference of BSPMR, 2026.

BACKGROUND

A platform for rehabilitation medicine in Bangladesh

The 1st Annual Scientific Conference is the first flagship scientific event of the Bangladesh Society of Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation, held on 5–6 December 2026 at Shahid Abu Sayed International Convention Center, Bangladesh Medical University, Shahbag. It brings together physiatrists, trainees and allied rehabilitation professionals from across the country and abroad.
