ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

Where rehabilitation science meets everyday practice

The 1st Annual Scientific Conference of BSPMR brings together physiatrists, physiotherapists, occupational therapists, trainees and allied professionals to share evidence, skills and experience in physical medicine and rehabilitation.

Organised by the Bangladesh Society of Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation — serving the specialty since 1995.
